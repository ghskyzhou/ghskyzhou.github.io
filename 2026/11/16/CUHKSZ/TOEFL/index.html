New TOEFL

Preface

This blog is intended to record my New TOEFL learning, especially Writing Part. The format may be not so formal but I believe the contents are helpful.

Learning Materials

  • ChatGPT Website Version (Plus Premium)
  • 不背单词 APP with free TOEFL words
  • ETS Official Practice Materials with full PDF contents and audio to download
  • Some random Collocations cards from Ankiweb
  • Baidu Translation, optimized for Chinese English learner

Should be informed that I have finshed about 3000 TOEFL words memorizing from the total 4200 words of 不背单词 APP in 3 months (about 35 hours in total). To myself, there are nearly about 2/3 of the words that I have known before, but the other 1/3 words are really painful to memorize. They are like rearrange and combination of 26 English letters, which is really hard. After then I started to do the following practice.

So far, ChatGPT pointed out my biggest problem is that the language is NOT NATURAL ENOUGH. Perhaps I can get the input kinda well, but my poor output, including speaking and writing, will definitely hold my back. My solution is memorizing some natural collocations and try my best to use them in practices.

It’s like, can Chinese native speakers become English native speakers? Apparently, NO. But I believe that by our efforts, we can be as close as possible to a English native speaker. All we have to do is to forcibly train ourselves to learn English everyday. Real learning happens when you struggle.

Student Practice 2

Writing

Email

My Answer:

Comment:

3/5 (near 4 but not so stable)

  1. Content does not expand enough, like I just rewrote the information that given by statement again, but didn’t point out the issues of the restaurant and the reaction of the teammates.

  2. Exsisting multiple unnatural expression, especially the sentence structure, adjective words component and connection words using.

Correction:

  1. Deleting the Hello!

  2. Recently, I have taken… has unnatural tense, should be changed to We recently took the team to the restaurant you recommended. with simple past tense.

  3. But actually the food was…, the tone is kinda stiff, and the content is empty, should be changed to Unfortunately, the food didn’t meet our expectations, and the service was quite slow., much better than but actually.

  4. Due to this, our teammates got a bit disappointed., which is still kinda stiff, perhaps As a result, serveral team members were disappointed with the experience is better, using as a result and team members

  5. However, the restaurant may not be… shows the unmatching comparison object, and the collocation is still unnatural. It should be changed to Although the restaurant didn’t live up to the online reviews, we have found another place worth trying., using the natural live up to the reviews.

  6. There is an old but… has obvious grammar and collocation issue, can be changed to There is a well-established Japanese restaurant near Stanford that is known for its good food and service.

  7. Thank you! Yours, XXX can just be like Best, XXX, since writing Email to coworker, best is more natural and simple.

ChatGPT’s 5/5 rewrite:

Why it is closer to 5/5?

  • P1 tells the background straightforwardly with kind tone, not blaming Kevin.
  • P2 not only describes the issue, but also points out the effect of slow service to lunch break.
  • P3 provides the exchanging plan, and believable recommending reasons.
  • P4 invites Kevin to take part in the discussion by What do you think, suitable for on-work Emails.
  • Natural languages, sipmle words and not building complex senctence by means.

Five Good Expressions:

  1. meet one’s expectations 达到某人的预期
  2. As a result 因此，结果是
  3. live up to 不辜负，达到某种评价
  4. be known for … 因……而出名
  5. highly recommend 强烈推荐
